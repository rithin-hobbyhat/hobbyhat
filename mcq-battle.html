Quick Guide

How to Play Tech Battle on Smartphone & PC

Designed for frictionless online learning. Whether you are revising a single chapter or taking a complete mock exam:

1

1. Select Subject(s)

Pick one or several subjects simultaneously (AMVI, Transmission, Chassis, Electricals, etc.) or click "Select All" to practice across all 6,498 questions.

2

2. Choose Your Mode

Pick Random Order for a genuine shuffled mock exam challenge, or As Per Order to study questions sequentially.

3

3. Instant Feedback & Explanations

Select any choice: correct answers turn green (✔), wrong choices turn red (❌), and detailed explanations instantly appear with KaTeX formulas.

4

4. Retest Mistakes Only

At the end of your session, inspect your scorecard, review answers, and click "Retest Mistakes Only" to achieve 100% mastery!

🚨 Smartphone Exclusive

Pocket Study Mode (Background Siren Reminders)

Busy schedule? Turn on Pocket Study Mode on your smartphone. Even when your phone screen is off or the app is minimized, a 20-second industrial siren and vibration will alert you at your chosen interval (3, 5, or 10 min) to answer the next technical question!
