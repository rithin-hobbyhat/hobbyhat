📚 how2bin Guide: 4 Standard Bins & 5S Method

🗑️ The 4 Standard Real-World Waste Bins

🌱 Green Bin (Wet / Biodegradable Waste)

Food leftovers, vegetable & fruit peels, garden trimmings, tea leaves, and coffee grounds. Rich nutrient source for clean compost!

♻️ Blue Bin (Dry / Recyclable Waste)

Clean paper, cardboard boxes, plastic packaging and bottles, glass jars, and metal cans/foils. Must be empty, clean, and dry!

☣️ Red Bin (Sanitary Waste)

Used diapers, sanitary napkins, band-aids, runny-nose tissues, and soiled bandages. Rule: Wrap securely in paper before disposal!

⚠️ Black Bin (Special Care / Domestic Hazardous)

Batteries, bulbs, fluorescent tube lights, expired medicines, paint cans, and small e-waste. Keep sealed and tape battery poles!

🏥 Indian Biomedical Waste Color Standard (Task 2)

☣️ Yellow Bin (Anatomical & Soiled Waste)

Human anatomical waste, blood-soaked cotton, soiled dressings, lab cultures, chemical waste, and expired medicines. Incinerated or autoclaved safely.

🧴 Red Bin (Recyclable Contaminated Plastics)

Contaminated plastics: IV sets, tubing, catheters, syringes without needles, and rubber gloves. Autoclaved and recycled.

🗡️ White Container (Puncture-Proof Metallic Sharps)

Metallic sharps: needles, scalpels, fixed-needle syringes, and lancets. Puncture-resistant container prevents needle-stick injuries.

🧪 Blue Bin (Glassware & Body Implants)

Glass medicine vials, ampoules, microscope glass slides, and metallic orthopedic bone screws/implants.

📐 The 5S Workplace & Home Method

  • 1. Seiri (Sort): Clear clutter. Separate what you need from expired hazards and junk.
  • 2. Seiton (Set in Order): Outline and arrange every bin and safety tool in its designated spot.
  • 3. Seiso (Shine): Clean surfaces and inspect equipment so spills and cracks are caught early.
  • 4. Seiketsu (Standardize): Use clear color-coding and official hazard pictograms everywhere.
  • 5. Shitsuke (Sustain): Maintain daily tidiness and safety discipline even when nobody is looking!
