Quick Guide

How to Play & Build Circuits

Whether you're completing curriculum challenge levels or inventing custom circuits in the free sandbox, here is how to use the studio:

1

Pick & Place Components

Drag cells, batteries, switches, lamps, buzzers, and transformers from the left drawer directly onto your breadboard.

2

Draw Flexible Wires

Click and drag from any component terminal pin. Move your cursor to curve around obstacles and snap onto destination pins.

3

Create Solder Joints

Need parallel branches? Tap or drag any wire onto an existing wire to snap and create a solid solder junction node.

4

Test & Measure Live

Observe real electron current flow, inspect voltages with the digital multimeter, and toggle switches to see your circuit come alive!
